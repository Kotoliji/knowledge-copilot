# IT Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and stored in the company password manager. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN, the cloud consoles and the HR portal. Hardware security keys are issued to all engineers and administrators.

## Devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption, automatic screen lock after 5 minutes and the endpoint protection agent installed. Personal devices may access email only through the managed mobile app. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## Access requests

Access to production systems is requested through the IT portal and requires approval from the system owner. Production access is reviewed every 90 days and automatically revoked after 30 days of inactivity.

## Reporting security incidents

Suspected phishing, malware, or data leaks must be reported immediately to security@company.example or via the "Report incident" button in Slack. Do not forward suspicious emails to colleagues.

## Software and cloud services

New SaaS tools must pass a security review before company data can be stored in them. Installing unapproved software on company laptops is prohibited. Open-source libraries used in products must have a permissive license (MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD).
