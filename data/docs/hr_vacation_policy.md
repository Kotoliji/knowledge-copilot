# Vacation and Leave Policy

## Annual vacation allowance

Every full-time employee receives 24 paid vacation days per calendar year. Part-time employees receive vacation days pro-rated by their contracted hours. Vacation days accrue monthly at a rate of 2 days per month starting from the first day of employment.

## Carry-over rule

Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used before March 31, otherwise they expire. Days beyond the 5-day limit are forfeited and are not paid out, except where local law requires otherwise.

## Requesting vacation

Vacation requests are submitted in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance for absences longer than 3 days. Shorter absences require 3 working days notice. Managers must approve or reject requests within 2 working days.

## Sick leave

Employees are entitled to up to 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for absences of more than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not reduce the vacation allowance.

## Parental leave

Parental leave is granted for 16 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver. Requests must be submitted at least 8 weeks before the planned start date.

## Public holidays

The company observes the public holidays of the country where the employee is contracted. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, no substitute day is granted unless local law requires it.
