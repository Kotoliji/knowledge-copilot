# Product FAQ: Nimbus Analytics Platform

## Pricing plans

Nimbus Analytics has three plans. Starter costs 49 USD per month and includes 3 users and 100,000 events per month. Team costs 199 USD per month with 15 users and 2 million events per month. Enterprise pricing is custom and includes unlimited users, SSO, and a dedicated support engineer.

## Data retention

Starter plan data is retained for 90 days. Team plan data is retained for 13 months. Enterprise customers can configure retention from 13 months up to 7 years.

## Integrations

Nimbus integrates natively with Snowflake, BigQuery, PostgreSQL, Salesforce and HubSpot. Custom sources can be connected through the REST ingestion API or the Python SDK. Webhooks are available on Team and Enterprise plans.

## Security and compliance

All data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. Enterprise customers may choose data residency in the EU (Frankfurt) or the US (Virginia).

## Support

Starter customers get email support with a 2 business day response target. Team customers get email and chat support with a 1 business day target. Enterprise customers get 24/7 support with a 1 hour response target for critical issues.

## Cancellation and refunds

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and remain active until the end of the billing period. Annual plans are refundable within the first 30 days. After 30 days no refund is issued but the subscription can be downgraded at renewal.
